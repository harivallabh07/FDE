# Free Learning Resources

**How to use these links:** choose the primary video for the topic, follow along in your own editor/notebook, pause before each implementation, and complete the assignment in the matching numbered module. All video/course links below are free to access at the time of curation; platform account requirements or future availability may change.

## Study rule
Do not binge-watch. For every 30–60 minutes of video, spend at least the same amount of time coding, testing, or writing a technical explanation. Commit your work to the matching module folder.
